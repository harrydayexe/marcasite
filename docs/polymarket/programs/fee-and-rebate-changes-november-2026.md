# Upcoming Fee and Rebate Changes

> Prepare for the Polymarket DeFi fee and rebate changes taking effect November 20, 2026 at 23:59 UTC.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Effective Friday, November 20, 2026 at 23:59 UTC.** These changes apply to
> Polymarket DeFi on [Polymarket.com](https://polymarket.com). The current fee
> and rebate rules remain in effect until that time.

## Taker Fees

Taker fees will continue to use the same formula:

```text theme={null}
fee = shares × feeRate × price × (1 − price)
```

`shares` is the filled quantity, `price` is the fill price in dollars (for
example, `0.40` for 40¢), and `feeRate` is the category coefficient below.
The coefficients are inputs to the formula, not flat percentages of trade
value.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Makers continue to pay no platform trading fee.

| Category | Fee Coefficient |
| - | - |
| Crypto — BTC 5-minute and 15-minute up/down | 0.05 |
| Crypto — all other markets | 0.01 |
| All sports, including NFL and college football | 0.05 |
| Finance | 0.05 |
| Tech | 0.05 |
| Politics — elections | 0.05 |
| Politics — excluding elections and geopolitics | 0.01 |
| Economics | 0.01 |
| Culture | 0.01 |
| Weather | 0.01 |
| Mentions | 0.01 |
| Geopolitics | 0 |
| Combos | 0.05 |
| Other / General | 0.05 |

Geopolitics remains fee-free. Combos keep their own fee coefficient, regardless
of the categories of their underlying legs.

## Taker Rebates

The Bronze and Silver tiers will be removed. Accounts with less than
\$200,000 in 30-day taker weighted volume will earn no taker rebate.

The rebate rate on the Gold taker rebate tier moves to 10%, Platinum to 25%,
and Diamond to 40%. Obsidian will stay at 50%, and a new Onyx tier will pay 60%.

| Tier | 30-Day Taker Weighted Volume | Rebate on Taker Fees Paid |
| - | - | - |
| None | Under \$200,000 | 0% |
| Gold | \$200,000 to under \$1 million | 10% |
| Platinum | \$1 million to under \$4 million | 25% |
| Diamond | \$4 million to under \$10 million | 40% |
| Obsidian | \$10 million to under \$75 million | 50% |
| Onyx (new) | \$75 million and above | 60% |

Price weighting stays; category multipliers are removed. Each fee-paying taker
fill contributes `shares × price × (1 − price)` to weighted volume. Your taker
tier uses the sum over the last 30 days. This differs from dollar trading
volume (`shares × price`). Please note that with no category multipliers
moving forward, your tier may change even if you trade the same amount.

The Gold, Platinum, Diamond, and Obsidian weighted-volume thresholds stay the
same. One-time level-up bonuses will end.

## Maker Rebates

Tiered maker rebates will replace the daily market-wide rebate pools. Your
maker tier will depend on your maker weighted volume over the last 30 days.
Each eligible maker fill will earn your tier's percentage of the taker fee it
generates.

Maker fills count only toward your maker tier; taker fills count only toward
your taker tier.

| Tier | 30-Day Maker Weighted Volume | Rebate on Taker Fees Generated by Your Fills |
| - | - | - |
| None | Under \$200,000 | 0% |
| Tier 1 | \$200,000 to under \$1 million | 3% |
| Tier 2 | \$1 million to under \$4 million | 7% |
| Tier 3 | \$4 million to under \$10 million | 10% |
| Tier 4 | \$10 million and above | 15% |

Maker weighted volume uses the same formula as taker weighted volume. For
example, a Tier 4 maker whose eligible fills generate \$100 in taker fees earns
\$15 in maker rebates.

Combos and fee-free trades earn no maker rebates.
[Liquidity rewards](https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/liquidity-rewards.md) remain unchanged.

Until the changes take effect, refer to the current [Fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees.md),
[Taker Rebate Program](https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/taker-rebates.md), and [Maker Rebates
Program](https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/maker-rebates.md) pages.
